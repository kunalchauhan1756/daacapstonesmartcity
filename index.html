Dijkstra’s algorithm

Keeps a min-heap of locations ordered by distance from the start. It repeatedly takes the closest unsettled location and relaxes its roads. Runs in O((V + E) log V).

Needs non-negative road weights, which suits distances and travel times.

Bellman-Ford

Relaxes every road, repeats up to V − 1 passes, and stops early when a pass changes nothing. Runs in O(V · E) in the worst case.

Handles negative weights and detects negative cycles, such as a toll rebate that loops back on itself.
